# Leagues, seasons, relegation, trophies

**Ladder (top to bottom):** C2 Champions League · C1 Europa League · B2 Bundesliga · B1 2. Bundesliga · A2 DFB-Pokal · A1 3. Liga.

**League Points (LP)** = known words + form + bonuses. Known words dominate.
- *Known words (squad value)*: each word counts by stage (new 0, learning 0.3, young 0.7, mastered 1). A grammar item counts as 2 words.
- *League entry = the usual vocabulary size of that level* (cumulative): A1 about 650 (start league, entry 0), A2 1,300, B1 2,400 (the first three follow the Goethe-Institut word lists), B2 about 4,000, C1 about 7,000, C2 about 10,000 (common estimates; no exam board publishes official counts above B1).
- *Form* (-100...+150) changes every matchday (one ISO week): win +20 (+30 if you practised all 7 days), draw 0, loss -15 (under 50% of the weekly target), no-show -30. The first week never costs points.
- *Bonuses*: season exam grades (gold +40, silver +25, bronze +10).

**Season** = 4 matchdays. At the final whistle: LP at or above the next threshold = promotion; LP more than a small buffer below your own league's threshold = relegation; otherwise you stay. The weekly target is 5 × your daily goal; you need 4 active days for a win.

**Trophies:** season exam ("Saisonfinale", 20 questions, 70/80/90% for bronze/silver/gold), promotion trophy, title trophy (3+ wins and no loss in a season). All are in the cabinet (Table → Trophy cabinet). Missed exams can be played for two seasons.

**Football statistics:** shots = answers, goals = right first time, on target = goals + near misses, xG = sum of (right+1)/(right+wrong+2) per card before the answer, clean sheets = error-free sessions, minutes = time spent.

**Language:** Settings → Sprache / Language (English or Deutsch). Card data (your words) is never translated.

## Career path and goals (version 2.4.0)

The league ladder is long, so there is now a **career path** inside it: 41 small stages that follow your LP from the street up to the top.
Examples: Bolzplatz (street football) → Hinterhof-Turnier → Sonntagsliga → Kreisliga → Amateurliga → … → Mittelklasse-Team → Meisterschaftself → Champions-League-Anwärter.
Each stage shows up on **Table → League** with a progress bar and a toast when you reach it.

**Goals** (31 small objectives such as "first practice session", "3 day streak", "100 answers", "combo of 10") pay a few extra LP once each (4 to 25 LP). They sit in the bonus part of LP, so they never replace learning words. Each goal can only be paid once.

**Records** (Table → Statistics): longest and current streak, best day, best week, best combo, clean sessions, answers, words met and mastered, stars, Tactics Room talks, badges, duel record and best duel win streak.
